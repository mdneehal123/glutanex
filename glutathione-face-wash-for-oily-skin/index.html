Is a glutathione face wash good for oily skin?

It can be, if it is a gentle foaming gel. Oily skin likes a wash that clears oil without leaving skin tight. Gluta Boost Face Wash is a foaming gel made for all skin types.

How many times a day should oily skin be washed?

Twice a day is right for most people, plus a rinse after heavy sweating or the gym. Washing more often can dry skin and make it feel oilier later.

Does a face wash stop pimples?

A wash helps keep pores clear but does not treat acne on its own. For frequent pimples, see a dermatologist.

Oily skin needs a wash that clears oil and sweat without stripping. Stripping makes skin feel tight and can lead to more shine by afternoon. In Indian heat and humidity, a gentle foaming gel twice a day works for most people.

What to look for

  • A gel or foaming texture that rinses off cleanly.
  • Soothing ingredients such as niacinamide, aloe or panthenol.
  • No gritty scrub particles for daily use.

How to use it, step by step

  1. Wet your face with lukewarm water, not hot.
  2. Take one pump, foam it between wet palms first.
  3. Massage gently for 30 to 60 seconds, a little longer on the forehead, nose and chin where oil collects.
  4. Rinse well along the hairline and jaw, where residue hides.
  5. Pat dry with a clean towel and apply a light gel moisturiser within a minute or two.

Oily skin in winter

Oily skin can still feel tight in December and January, especially in north India or with a room heater. Keep the same face wash but use lukewarm water, skip a third wash in the day, and use a slightly richer moisturiser at night. If your T-zone stays oily but cheeks get dry, see combination skin.

Oily and acne-prone: what a face wash can and cannot do

A face wash is on the skin for under a minute, so it mainly cleans. It helps keep pores clear but does not replace a leave-on product for pimples. If you get painful or frequent breakouts, see a dermatologist. For marks left behind, see glutathione for acne marks; and if a new product seems to cause bumps, read glutathione face wash side effects.

Mistakes that make oily skin worse

  • Washing 4 to 5 times a day.
  • Skipping moisturiser: dry, tight skin often looks oilier later.
  • Hot water and hard rubbing.
  • Heavy hair oil running onto the forehead.

Frequently asked questions

Should oily skin use moisturiser?

Yes. A light gel moisturiser helps skin stay balanced and comfortable.

Can men with oily skin use it?

Yes. It is for anyone.

Foam or gel: which is better for oily skin?

Both work; many people with oily skin prefer a gel that foams, like Gluta Boost.

Can I use a glutathione face wash twice a day?

Yes. Morning and night is right for most oily skin. Add a plain water rinse after the gym or a sweaty commute rather than a third wash.

Does a glutathione face wash reduce oil?

It removes oil that is on the skin at the time of washing, so skin looks less shiny for a while. It does not change how much oil your skin makes; a light moisturiser and not over-washing help more over the day.

Can I use it with a salicylic acid face wash?

Use one or the other at each wash. Some people use a salicylic acid wash at night and a gentle wash in the morning. If your night cream already has salicylic acid, like the GlutaNex Night Cream, keep the face wash gentle.
