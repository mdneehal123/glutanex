Which cream is no. 1 for skin whitening in India?

There is no officially "no. 1" skin whitening cream in India, and no safe cream changes natural skin colour. For tan and uneven tone, look for gentle brightening ingredients such as glutathione, niacinamide and vitamin C, avoid creams that lighten within days, and use sunscreen daily.

Creams that work "too fast" may contain steroids, hydroquinone or mercury. Read glutathione and skin whitening: what is true.

Is glutathione cream good for skin whitening?

Glutathione cream can help tan and uneven tone look lighter and more even over 8 to 12 weeks, especially with daily sunscreen, but it will not change your natural skin colour. Small studies show modest, gradual effects. Be wary of any cream promising fast or permanent whitening.

Is there a good glutathione cream under ₹200?

Some basic glutathione creams cost under ₹200, but they are usually small tubes with short ingredient lists and little or unstated glutathione. Compare price per gram and the full formula, and avoid unbranded creams with no address or batch number, which may contain banned ingredients.

The 7-point checklist

  1. Glutathione is clearly listed in the full ingredient list printed on the pack.
  2. It has useful partners. Niacinamide (vitamin B3), vitamin C, hyaluronic acid and ceramides support even tone, hydration and the skin barrier.
  3. Night or day use is clear. Creams with retinol or exfoliating acids belong at night.
  4. Realistic claims. "Brighter-looking" and "more even" are honest; "fair in 7 days" is not.
  5. Safe packaging. Sealed, with batch number, manufacturing and expiry dates.
  6. A real seller. Company name, address, phone or WhatsApp, and a return policy.
  7. Fits your skin type (see below).

What the evidence can and cannot tell you

The best-known topical study used a 2% oxidised glutathione lotion for 10 weeks (Watanabe et al., 2014) and found a modest improvement in skin tone. A 2025 systematic review (Khanna et al., J Clin Aesthet Dermatol 2025) called topical glutathione promising but under-studied. So be wary of any brand that claims clinical proof of dramatic results.
