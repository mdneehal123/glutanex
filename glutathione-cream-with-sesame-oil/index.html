Glutathione cream with sesame oil (til ka tel): face or body, and the right order

Can you use til ka tel with glutathione cream? On the body in winter, yes; on the face, only a drop over the cream on very dry nights. Which sesame oil, the right order and who should skip it.

Can I use sesame oil with glutathione cream?

Yes, but layer them instead of mixing. Apply the glutathione cream first on clean, dry skin, wait a few minutes, then use a little plain cold-pressed sesame oil on top. It suits dry body skin in winter best; on the face, keep it to one or two drops on dry areas.

Is til ka tel good for the face?

Sesame oil is fairly rich, so it suits dry skin better than oily or acne-prone skin. Use plain, cold-pressed oil, not dark toasted cooking oil, and only a drop or two. If you get new bumps within a week or two, stop using it on the face.

Can sesame oil remove tan?

No. Sesame oil softens dry skin, but it does not remove tan or change skin colour. Tan fades slowly over weeks with daily sunscreen, shade and steady skincare.

You can use sesame oil (til ka tel, also called gingelly oil or nallennai) with glutathione cream, as long as you layer them and do not mix the oil into the jar. Apply the cream first, then a little plain cold-pressed sesame oil on top; it works best on dry body skin in winter, while on the face one or two drops over dry areas is plenty.

Til ka tel is part of many Indian homes: oil massage before a bath, winter care in the north, and weekly oil baths in the south. It is a rich, slightly heavy oil that slows water loss from dry skin. It is not a brightening ingredient, and it does not change what your cream does or your natural skin colour.

Which sesame oil to use

  • Cold-pressed, plain sesame oil (light golden, mild smell): the one to use on skin.
  • Dark toasted sesame oil (brown, strong nutty smell, sold for cooking and Chinese food): skip it on the face; the smell is strong and it can irritate.
  • "Til oil" blends with perfume, colour or "fairness" herbs: skip them. No oil changes skin colour, and added fragrance raises the chance of irritation.
  • Check the seal and the use-by date, and keep the bottle closed and out of the sun; old oil smells sharp or rancid.

The right order: cream first, oil last

  1. Wash or bathe with a gentle cleanser such as Gluta Boost Face Wash for the face, and pat dry.
  2. Wait until the skin is fully dry, especially on nights you use a retinol cream.
  3. Apply glutathione cream: a pea-sized amount for the face, or your body cream on arms, legs, elbows and knees.
  4. Wait 5 to 10 minutes for it to settle.
  5. Warm a little sesame oil between your palms and press it over the dry areas. For the face, one or two drops only, on the cheeks and jawline, never the oily T-zone.
  6. Sunscreen every morning on the face and on any body skin that sees the sun.

Oil massage before a bath: also fine

Many families do a til oil massage before bathing, especially on winter weekends. That is a separate step and works well with a glutathione routine:

  1. Massage sesame oil on the body (and face, if your skin is dry) 15 to 30 minutes before the bath.
  2. Bathe with lukewarm, not very hot, water and a gentle wash.
  3. Pat dry and apply your cream afterwards.

Leave the face out of the massage if it is oily or breaks out easily. For body care, see glutathione for underarms and body and dark knees and elbows.

Winter is when til ka tel helps most

From November to February, much of north and central India gets cold, dry air, and room heaters and hot baths dry skin further. That is when shins, elbows, hands and heels crack, and when a rich oil over a body cream is most useful. In humid coastal cities, or in the monsoon, most people need far less oil, or none on the face. Read glutathione cream in winter for the rest of a winter routine.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Using dark cooking sesame oil on the face.
  • Applying oil first, then cream; the cream spreads less evenly.
  • Using a palmful on the face "for glow"; it ends up on the pillow and can block pores.
  • Going into the sun after oiling without sunscreen.
  • Expecting oil to remove tan. Compare other oils in mustard oil, coconut oil and almond oil with glutathione cream.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if your skin stays cracked, itchy or scaly despite moisturising, if a new oil or cream causes a rash or swelling, or if dark patches keep spreading.
