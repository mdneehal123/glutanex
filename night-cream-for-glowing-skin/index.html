Which night cream is best for glowing skin?

The best night cream for glowing skin is one that suits your skin type and has proven brightening ingredients such as niacinamide, vitamin C, glutathione or retinol, plus hydrators like hyaluronic acid and ceramides. Use it nightly and wear sunscreen every morning, or the glow will not last.

Do night creams really make skin glow?

Yes, in a modest way. A good night cream keeps skin hydrated, which makes it look smoother and brighter by morning, and active ingredients can help dullness and dark marks look lighter over 8 to 12 weeks. No cream changes your natural skin colour.

Can I use a night cream every day?

Most plain hydrating night creams can be used every night. Creams with retinol or exfoliating acids should start 2 to 3 nights a week and build up slowly, with sunscreen every morning.

A night cream gives a real glow in two ways. First, it keeps skin hydrated overnight, so it looks smoother and plumper in the morning. Second, active ingredients can slowly help dullness, tan and dark marks look lighter. The right cream depends on your skin type, and none of it works without sunscreen in the day.

How to apply a night cream for the best glow

  1. Remove makeup and sunscreen, then wash with a gentle face wash such as Gluta Boost Face Wash.
  2. Pat dry and wait a minute or two, especially if your cream has retinol or acids.
  3. Use a pea-sized amount for the whole face. Dot it on the forehead, cheeks and chin, then spread gently.
  4. Avoid the eye corners and lips with active night creams.
  5. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or higher. This is what keeps the glow. See glutathione cream with sunscreen.

How long it takes

  • First week: skin feels softer and looks more hydrated in the morning.
  • 4 to 6 weeks: less dullness and a more even look.
  • 8 to 12 weeks: tan and dark marks may look lighter, with daily sunscreen.

Older dark spots and melasma take longer and may need a dermatologist. See glutathione cream results.

Claims to ignore

  • "Fair in 7 days" or "permanent whitening": skin does not renew that fast, and no cream changes your natural colour.
  • "100% safe, no side effects": every active can irritate some people.
  • No ingredient list or maker address on the pack: skip it. See glutathione side effects.

Small habits that add to the glow

  • 7 to 8 hours of sleep; the morning puffiness and dullness really are worse without it.
  • Change pillow covers twice a week if you are acne-prone.
  • Drink enough water and eat fruits and vegetables. See glutathione-rich foods.
