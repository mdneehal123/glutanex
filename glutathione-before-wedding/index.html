Glutathione before your wedding: an honest 12-week plan

When to start, a week-by-week plan, and why to skip pre-wedding glutathione injections.

By the GlutaNex team · Updated 11 October 2026 · 2 sources cited

Key takeaways
  • Start a glutathione cream 10–12 weeks before the wedding, slowly at first, with daily sunscreen.
  • Do not start new products, peels or bleach in the final two weeks.
  • Skip glutathione injections and IV glow drips; they lack trials and carry warnings.

Is glutathione good before a wedding?

A glutathione cream can help tan and uneven tone look brighter if you start 8–12 weeks before the day and wear sunscreen daily. It will not change your natural skin colour, and glutathione injections, often sold as pre-wedding packages, are not recommended.

How many weeks before the wedding should I start glutathione cream?

Start 10–12 weeks before if you can. That leaves two weeks to build up slowly, about eight weeks for gradual results, and time to stop anything that irritates. Starting a new active cream in the last two weeks is risky.

Are glutathione injections safe before marriage?

There are no proper clinical trials showing glutathione injections are safe or effective for skin lightening, and health regulators have warned about them. Drips given outside a hospital can also carry infection risk. It is best to skip them, especially before a big day.

Read the warning: US FDA warning, 2015.

A week-by-week plan

Weeks beforeWhat to do
12–11Patch test for 24 hours. Start the night cream 2–3 nights a week. Sunscreen every morning from now on.
10–9If your skin is comfortable, use it every other night.
8–3Every night if your skin is happy. Keep up sunscreen, sleep and water; avoid new products.

For grooms too

The same plan works for men. Shaving can make actives sting, so apply the night cream on shaved areas only once any razor irritation has settled, and use a fragrance-free moisturiser after shaving.

Only 4 weeks to go (or Diwali is close)?

A cream can make skin look fresher and smoother within a few weeks, but tan and spots need longer. Focus on daily sunscreen, a gentle cleanser like Gluta Boost Face Wash, sleep and a steady routine rather than anything strong or new.

What to avoid before the wedding

  • Glutathione injections and IV "glow drips".
  • Bleach creams and unlabelled "fairness" creams, which may contain steroids or mercury.
  • A first-ever chemical peel in the final month.
  • Lemon, baking soda or toothpaste on the face.
