Glutathione with niacinamide and vitamin C

How these ingredients work together in a routine.

By the GlutaNex team · Updated 11 October 2026 · 2 sources cited

On this page
  1. How they differ
  2. A simple way to layer
  3. Keep it gentle
  4. Why niacinamide and glutathione pair well
  5. Can I add vitamin C and retinol too?

Yes, glutathione can be used with niacinamide and vitamin C. They are often combined in products made for brighter, more even-looking skin.

How they differ

IngredientUsed forGood to know
GlutathioneAntioxidant support, brighter-looking skinWorks gradually
Niacinamide (vitamin B3)Even-looking tone, smoother texture, skin barrierMay tingle briefly at first
Vitamin CAntioxidant, brightnessBest in the morning, under sunscreen

A simple way to layer

  • Morning: cleanser → vitamin C (optional) → sunscreen.
  • Night: cleanser → glutathione cream (with niacinamide if your product has it).

Keep it gentle

Add one new product at a time, patch test, and avoid stacking strong acids or retinol on the same night. Read side effects and safe use.

Why niacinamide and glutathione pair well

Niacinamide helps reduce the transfer of pigment to the skin's surface cells and strengthens the barrier, while glutathione works earlier in melanin production and adds antioxidant protection. Together they target uneven tone from two directions, and both are generally gentle.

Can I add vitamin C and retinol too?

Yes, with care. Vitamin C is ideal in the morning under sunscreen. Retinol belongs at night and should be introduced slowly. If your glutathione cream already contains retinol, such as GlutaNex Glutathione Night Cream, do not add another retinol product.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use glutathione and niacinamide together every day?

Yes. They are often combined in one product and are generally gentle.

Can I use glutathione with vitamin C?

Yes. Vitamin C helps keep glutathione active. Use vitamin C in the morning under sunscreen.
