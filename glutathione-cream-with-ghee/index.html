Glutathione cream with ghee: can you use both on your face?

Can you put desi ghee on your face with glutathione cream? When a dab helps dry winter lips and patches, why not to cover a retinol cream with it, and who should skip ghee on the face.

Can I apply ghee with glutathione cream?

Yes, but only in small amounts and in the right order: the cream first on clean skin, then a tiny dab of pure ghee only on very dry spots such as cracked lips or flaky patches. Do not cover the whole face with ghee, and with a retinol night cream keep ghee for rest nights in the first weeks.

Should I apply ghee before or after glutathione cream?

After, and only where needed. Ghee is a rich fat that sits on top of the skin, so a layer underneath stops the cream from reaching the skin. The one exception is the lips: a dab of ghee there first helps keep a retinol cream off them.

Does ghee with glutathione make skin fair?

No. Ghee only softens very dry skin. A glutathione cream with daily sunscreen may help tan and dark marks look lighter slowly over 8 to 12 weeks. Neither changes your natural skin colour.

You can use ghee with glutathione cream, but only as a tiny dab on very dry spots, after the cream, never as a full-face layer. Ghee is a rich, heavy fat: it can soften cracked lips and flaky winter patches, but over the whole face it feels greasy, can clog pores and can make a retinol night cream feel stronger.

In many Indian homes, a little desi ghee goes on cracked lips, dry heels and the navel in winter, and some people now dab it on the face at night too. This guide explains when that is fine, when it is not, and how to fit it around a glutathione cream without breakouts or irritation.

The right order on a dry winter night

  1. Cleanse with a gentle face wash such as Gluta Boost Face Wash and pat the face almost dry.
  2. Lips first: dab a rice-grain amount of ghee on the lips so the cream does not spread onto them.
  3. Apply the glutathione cream as a thin, pea-sized layer over the face, away from the eyes, lips and nose corners.
  4. Let it settle for a few minutes.
  5. Press a tiny amount of ghee only onto flaky or cracked spots, not over the cream across the face.
  6. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or higher.

Never put ghee all over the face before the cream: a layer of fat underneath means the cream sits on top of grease. For general layering, see how to use glutathione cream and glutathione cream and moisturizer.

Using ghee cleanly

  • Use plain ghee with no salt, spices or added flavour. Keep a small separate bowl for your face, not the kitchen jar.
  • Take it out with a clean spoon or cotton bud, not fingers that have touched your face.
  • Throw it out if it smells sharp or sour; rancid fat irritates skin.
  • A rice-grain amount covers both lips. More only means a greasy pillow.
  • Do not use ghee to "seal in" home remedies like lemon, toothpaste or baking soda.

What to expect, honestly

A dab of ghee can make cracked lips and flaky spots feel softer by morning. It does not fade dark spots, tan or acne marks, and it does not make a glutathione cream work faster. Any gradual change in tone comes from steady use of the cream plus daily sunscreen over 8 to 12 weeks; read how long glutathione takes to work.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if dry patches crack and bleed, itch a lot, look red and scaly around the nose and eyebrows, or keep coming back despite moisturising, or if your lips stay cracked for weeks. These may need a diagnosis, not more layers.
