Glutathione night cream: how to choose and use one

Why night is the best time for glutathione, what to look for, and options for oily and sensitive skin.

By the GlutaNex team · Updated 11 October 2026 · 2 sources cited

On this page
  1. Why use glutathione at night?
  2. What to look for
  3. Glutathione night cream for oily skin
  4. Glutathione night cream for sensitive skin
  5. How to apply
  6. Results to expect

A glutathione night cream is a leave-on cream with glutathione designed for your evening routine. Night is a good time for brightening care: there is no sun exposure, and creams with retinol or exfoliating acids must be used at night anyway.

Why use glutathione at night?

  • Skin repairs itself overnight, without sun and pollution.
  • Night creams can be richer and more nourishing.
  • Partners such as retinol and salicylic acid belong at night.

What to look for

IngredientWhy it helps at night
GlutathioneAntioxidant for brighter, more even-looking tone
NiacinamideEven tone and a stronger skin barrier
Hyaluronic acid and ceramidesOvernight hydration, less dryness from actives
RetinolSmoother texture; start slowly
PeptidesFirmer, smoother-looking skin

Glutathione night cream for oily skin

Choose a light, non-greasy texture. Niacinamide and salicylic acid help with shine and clogged pores. Apply a thin layer.

Glutathione night cream for sensitive skin

Patch test, start 2 to 3 nights a week, and avoid layering other actives. A soothing ingredient such as aloe vera helps. Stop if burning lasts.

How to apply

  1. Cleanse with a gentle face wash such as Gluta Boost Face Wash and pat dry.
  2. Use a pea-sized amount on face and neck, avoiding eyes and lips.
  3. Sunscreen every morning.

Results to expect

Softer skin within days; a brighter, more even look over 4 to 8 weeks; tan and marks lighter over 8 to 12 weeks with daily sunscreen. The topical study used a 2% glutathione lotion for 10 weeks (Watanabe et al., 2014).

Frequently asked questions

Is glutathione cream better at night or in the day?

Night is usually best, especially if the cream contains retinol or acids. Use sunscreen every morning.

Which glutathione night cream is good for oily skin?

Look for a light texture with niacinamide or salicylic acid, applied as a thin layer.
