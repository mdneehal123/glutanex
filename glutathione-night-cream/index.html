Glutathione night cream: how to choose and use one

Why night is the best time for glutathione, what to look for, and options for oily and sensitive skin.

By the GlutaNex team · Updated 5 November 2026 · 2 sources cited

Key takeaways
  • Night is ideal for glutathione creams, especially those with retinol or acids.
  • Look for niacinamide, hyaluronic acid and ceramides alongside glutathione.
  • Expect a brighter look in 4–8 weeks and lighter tan in 8–12 weeks with sunscreen.

A glutathione night cream is a leave-on cream with glutathione that you apply after cleansing at bedtime, then follow with sunscreen the next morning. Night is a good time for brightening care: there is no sun exposure, and creams with retinol or exfoliating acids must be used at night anyway.

Why use glutathione at night?

  • Skin repairs itself overnight, without sun and pollution.
  • Night creams can be richer and more nourishing.
  • Partners such as retinol and salicylic acid belong at night.

Glutathione night cream for oily skin

Choose a light, non-greasy texture. Niacinamide and salicylic acid help with shine and clogged pores. Apply a thin layer.

Glutathione night cream for sensitive skin

Patch test, start 2 to 3 nights a week, and avoid layering other actives. A soothing ingredient such as aloe vera helps. Stop if burning lasts.

How to apply

  1. Cleanse with a gentle face wash such as Gluta Boost Face Wash and pat dry.
  2. Use a pea-sized amount on face and neck, avoiding eyes and lips.
  3. Sunscreen every morning.

Results to expect

Softer skin within days; a brighter, more even look over 4 to 8 weeks; tan and marks lighter over 8 to 12 weeks with daily sunscreen. The topical study used a 2% glutathione lotion for 10 weeks (Watanabe et al., 2014).

How often to use it

If your night cream has retinol or salicylic acid, as ours does, start 2 to 3 nights a week for the first two weeks, then move to every other night and, if skin stays comfortable, every night. A plain glutathione cream without these can usually be used nightly from the start. More: can I use glutathione cream daily?

What not to layer on the same night

  • Another retinol or retinoid product.
  • Strong exfoliating acids (glycolic, lactic or a second salicylic product) or scrubs.
  • Raw lemon, baking soda or toothpaste "hacks".

A simple moisturiser on top is fine if your skin feels dry. See glutathione and salicylic acid and retinol vs glutathione.

Winter and summer at night

In dry North Indian winters or AC rooms, apply the cream on slightly damp skin and add a plain moisturiser if you feel tight. In humid summers and the monsoon, a thin layer is enough. Read glutathione cream in winter.

Who should check with a doctor first

If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, avoid retinol night creams unless your doctor says they are fine. If you use prescription creams for acne or pigmentation, ask before adding another active at night. Patchy brown pigment on both cheeks may be melasma, which needs a dermatologist.

Frequently asked questions

Is glutathione cream better at night or in the day?

Night is usually best, especially if the cream contains retinol or acids. Use sunscreen every morning.

Which glutathione night cream is good for oily skin?

Look for a light texture with niacinamide or salicylic acid, applied as a thin layer.

Can I apply glutathione night cream every night?

If it contains retinol or salicylic acid, start 2 to 3 nights a week and build up slowly. A gentle formula without them can usually be used nightly.

Should I wash my face in the morning after a glutathione night cream?

Yes. Rinse or use a gentle face wash, then apply sunscreen. Sun protection is what keeps tan and marks from coming back.

Can I use moisturiser over a glutathione night cream?

Yes, if your skin feels dry. Apply the night cream first, wait a minute, then a plain, fragrance-free moisturiser.
