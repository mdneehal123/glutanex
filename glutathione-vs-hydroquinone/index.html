Glutathione vs hydroquinone: which is better for your skin?

How glutathione and hydroquinone compare, who each suits, and whether to use them together.

By the GlutaNex team · Updated 11 October 2026 · 2 sources cited

Key takeaways
  • Hydroquinone is a strong prescription medicine in India; glutathione is a gentle cosmetic.
  • Use hydroquinone only under a dermatologist, for a limited time.
  • Some unbranded fairness creams hide hydroquinone and steroids; avoid them.

Which is better?

They are not alternatives for everyday care. Hydroquinone is a prescription treatment that a dermatologist may use for a limited time for conditions such as melasma. Glutathione is a gentle cosmetic ingredient for everyday brightening.

Watch out for hidden hydroquinone

Some unbranded "fairness" creams sold in India contain hydroquinone, often mixed with steroids, without saying so. Misuse can cause thinning, acne and patchy, darker skin. Avoid creams that lighten very fast or have no full ingredient list.

Prefer a gentle, clearly labelled routine: GlutaNex Glutathione Night Cream at night and sunscreen every morning.

Frequently asked questions

Is glutathione safer than hydroquinone?

For everyday use, yes. Glutathione is a gentle cosmetic ingredient, while hydroquinone is a strong prescription medicine that should be used only under a dermatologist.

Can I use hydroquinone without a prescription?

In India hydroquinone is a prescription medicine. Use it only if a dermatologist prescribes it, for the time they advise.
