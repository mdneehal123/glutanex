How long does glutathione cream take to show results?

Expect skin to feel smoother within 2 to 4 weeks, a fresher look in 4 to 8 weeks, and lighter-looking tan or marks in 8 to 12 weeks of nightly use with daily sunscreen. Studies on glutathione ran for 4 to 12 weeks.

Can glutathione show results in 7 days?

No honest product can promise visible lightening in 7 days. Skin renews over about a month, and pigment fades slowly. A cream that changes colour in days may contain steroids or bleach.

What the studies found

  • Topical: a 2% glutathione lotion showed a lower melanin index after 10 weeks (Watanabe et al., 2014).
  • Tablets: changes after 4 weeks at 500 mg a day (Arjinpathana & Asawanonda, 2012), and smaller, mostly non-significant changes over 12 weeks at 250 mg a day (Weschawalit et al., 2017).
  • A 2025 review: effects are modest and studies are short (Khanna et al., J Clin Aesthet Dermatol 2025).

Why some people see no results

  • No daily sunscreen, so new tan keeps replacing what fades.
  • Using it now and then instead of most nights.
  • Melasma, which needs a dermatologist (see glutathione for melasma).
  • Expecting a colour change: glutathione does not change natural skin colour.
  • Irritation from too much product or too many actives, which can leave new marks.
