How to use glutathione cream

A simple night routine, step by step.

By the GlutaNex team · Updated 25 October 2026 · 1 sources cited

Key takeaways
  • Night: cleanse, pat dry, apply a pea-sized amount to face and neck.
  • Morning: cleanse and use SPF 30 or higher sunscreen.
  • With retinol creams, start 2–3 nights a week and give the routine 8–12 weeks.

Use glutathione cream at night on clean, dry skin, and use sunscreen every morning. That's the whole routine.

Night routine

  1. Cleanse: wash your face with a gentle cleanser such as Gluta Boost Face Wash and pat dry.
  2. Apply: take a pea-sized amount and dot it on forehead, cheeks and chin, then smooth over face and neck. Avoid the eyes.
  3. Let it absorb: give it a few minutes before lying down.

Morning

Cleanse, then apply a broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher). Sun exposure keeps creating new tan and dark spots, so this step protects the work of the night.

Good pairings

  • Niacinamide: a gentle, well-studied ingredient often used with glutathione.
  • Vitamin C: another antioxidant; use it in the morning if you have it.
  • A simple moisturiser: if your skin feels dry.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Using a thick layer: more cream does not work faster.
  • Skipping sunscreen.
  • Layering extra strong products on top. The GlutaNex Night Cream already contains retinol and salicylic acid, so skip other retinol, AHA/BHA or peel products on the same night.
  • Going every night from day one: start 2 to 3 nights a week for the first two weeks.
  • Changing products every week: give one routine 6 to 8 weeks.

How much glutathione cream to use

A pea-sized amount covers the face; add a little more for the neck. Using more does not give faster results and can increase irritation.

A sample 8-week plan

WeeksNightMorning
1–2Cream on 2 to 3 nights, gentle cleanser every nightCleanser + sunscreen
3–4Cream every other night if comfortableCleanser + sunscreen
5–8Cream every nightCleanser + sunscreen, reapply outdoors

Glutathione cream for men

The routine is the same. Apply after shaving only once the skin has settled, as freshly shaved skin can sting.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply glutathione cream every day?

Yes, once your skin is used to it. With creams containing retinol, start 2 to 3 nights a week.

Should I wash off glutathione cream in the morning?

Yes, cleanse in the morning and apply sunscreen.
