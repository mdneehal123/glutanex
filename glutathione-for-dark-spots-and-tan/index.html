Glutathione for dark spots and tan

What helps tan and dark spots fade, and how long it takes.

By the GlutaNex team · Updated 11 October 2026 · 2 sources cited

On this page
  1. What causes them
  2. A routine that helps
  3. How long it takes
  4. When to see a doctor
  5. Tan vs dark spots vs melasma
  6. Home remedies to avoid

Glutathione creams can support the look of tan and dark spots fading, but sunscreen does most of the work. Without it, new tan and spots keep forming.

What causes them

  • Tan: the skin's response to UV light.
  • Marks after pimples: very common on Indian skin.
  • Sun spots: from years of sun exposure.
  • Melasma: patchy brown areas linked to hormones and sun; needs a dermatologist.

A routine that helps

  1. Sunscreen every morning, reapplied if you are outdoors for long.
  2. A glutathione cream every night, such as GlutaNex Glutathione Night Cream.
  3. No squeezing pimples, no harsh scrubs or lemon juice.

How long it takes

Tan usually lightens over a few weeks once you protect your skin. Dark spots take longer: often 8 to 12 weeks, and older spots may only lighten partly.

When to see a doctor

Patchy pigmentation on both cheeks, spots that change shape or colour, or no change after 3 months of care.

Tan vs dark spots vs melasma

Looks likeTypical timeline with care
TanEven darkening on sun-exposed areasA few weeks
Acne marksFlat brown spots where pimples were8–12 weeks or more
Sun spotsSmall brown spots, often on cheeks and handsMonths; may lighten only partly
MelasmaPatchy brown areas on both cheeks, forehead or upper lipNeeds a dermatologist

Home remedies to avoid

Lemon juice, baking soda, toothpaste and harsh scrubs can irritate the skin and make marks darker. Stick to gentle care and sunscreen.

Frequently asked questions

Does glutathione remove dark spots?

It may help dark spots look lighter over 8 to 12 weeks with daily sunscreen. Older spots may lighten only partly.

How fast does glutathione remove tan?

With daily sunscreen, a recent tan usually fades over a few weeks.
