Glutathione cream with coffee: scrub, pack and the right order

Can you use coffee with glutathione cream? Yes, as a separate rinse-off step: a soft coffee and curd pack, or a gentle coffee body scrub, then the cream on clean, dry skin. Never in the jar, never a hard face scrub on retinol nights, and an honest view of tan and dark circles.

Can I use coffee with glutathione cream?

Yes, as a separate step. Use coffee in a rinse-off pack or a body scrub, wash it off, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream on clean skin. Never stir coffee into the cream jar, and keep coffee scrubs off the face on nights you use a retinol or salicylic acid cream.

Is coffee scrub good for the face?

Coffee grounds are rough and uneven, so they can scratch the face, especially thin or sensitive skin. A smooth coffee pack mixed with curd or honey and rinsed off gently is a softer choice for the face. Keep the scrub for the body: arms, elbows, knees and heels.

Does coffee remove tan with glutathione cream?

No. A coffee pack or scrub can leave skin feeling smooth and looking fresher for a while, but it does not remove tan. Tan looks lighter slowly over weeks with daily sunscreen and shade, and a glutathione cream may help with that. Neither changes your natural skin colour.

The right order: coffee first, cream after

  1. Patch test the coffee mix on your inner arm or jawline and check the next day.
  2. Wash your face with a gentle face wash such as Gluta Boost Face Wash.
  3. Apply a thin coffee pack: one teaspoon of finely ground coffee powder with two teaspoons of plain curd or a little honey, mixed smooth.
  4. Leave it for about 10 minutes, before it dries hard.
  5. Rinse with lukewarm water using soft circles, no rubbing.
  6. Pat dry and wait a few minutes.
  7. Apply a thin layer of glutathione cream (on a night it suits, see below).
  8. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or higher.

Face vs body: where coffee scrub belongs

Coffee grounds have sharp, uneven edges. On thicker skin like elbows, knees, heels and the backs of arms, a gentle scrub once a week is fine. On the face, especially the cheeks and around the eyes, it can cause tiny scratches and redness. If you love the coffee feel, use instant coffee powder or very fine powder in a pack for the face, and keep coarse grounds for the body. For rough, dark-looking knees and elbows, see dark knees and elbows and body tan routine.

Coffee for dark circles and tan: an honest view

Caffeine is used in many eye creams to make the area look less puffy for a while. A home coffee pack is not the same thing, and it does not remove dark circles; keep grounds well away from the eyes and read glutathione for dark circles. Coffee also does not remove tan. Tan and marks look lighter slowly, over weeks, with sunscreen every morning and a steady routine; see glutathione for dark spots and tan.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if your skin burns, itches or breaks into bumps after a coffee pack or scrub, if scratches do not heal, or if dark patches on the cheeks keep spreading (this can be melasma, which needs a doctor).
