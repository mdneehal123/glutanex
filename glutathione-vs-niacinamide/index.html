Glutathione vs niacinamide: which is better for your skin?

How glutathione and niacinamide compare, who each suits, and whether to use them together.

By the GlutaNex team · Updated 11 October 2026 · 2 sources cited

Which is better?

They complement each other: glutathione acts early in melanin production, niacinamide reduces how much pigment reaches the skin surface. Together they target uneven tone from two directions, and both suit most skin types.

Using them together

Yes, they are often combined in one product, such as GlutaNex Glutathione Night Cream. Apply at night on clean skin and use sunscreen every morning.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use glutathione and niacinamide together?

Yes. They work in different ways on pigment and are both generally gentle, which is why many creams combine them.

Which is better for oily skin, glutathione or niacinamide?

Niacinamide is better known for oil control and pores; glutathione adds brightening. A light cream with both suits oily skin well.
