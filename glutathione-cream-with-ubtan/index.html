Glutathione cream with ubtan: how to use them before a wedding or festival

Can you use ubtan with glutathione cream? Yes, as a separate rinse-off pack first and the cream after, never mixed into the jar. A simple ubtan, the right order, haldi ceremony tips, mixes to skip and the night-only retinol rule.

Can I use ubtan and glutathione cream together?

Yes, but not mixed. Use ubtan as a rinse-off pack, wash it off gently, pat dry, wait about 10 minutes and then apply a thin layer of glutathione cream on its own. With a retinol night cream, keep ubtan and the cream on different nights for the first few weeks.

Should I apply cream after ubtan?

Yes. Ubtan with besan or multani mitti can leave skin feeling tight, so a moisturiser or night cream afterwards helps. Rinse the ubtan fully, pat dry, wait a few minutes, then apply a pea-sized amount of cream for the whole face.

How many days before a wedding should I use ubtan?

If you want to use ubtan, start 3 to 4 weeks before, once or twice a week, so you know your skin is fine with it. Do not try a new ubtan, scrub or strong cream in the last week before the function.

You can use ubtan and glutathione cream in the same routine, but not mixed together. Apply ubtan as a short rinse-off pack, wash it off gently, let your face dry, and then apply a thin layer of glutathione cream on its own, keeping the two on separate nights if your cream has retinol.

Ubtan is the traditional Indian paste of besan (gram flour), haldi (turmeric) and a liquid such as milk or rose water, sometimes with chandan, masoor dal or orange peel powder. It is at the centre of the haldi ceremony and of many pre-wedding and festival routines. It can leave skin feeling clean and smooth, but it needs the right order, gentle hands and honest expectations.

What ubtan can and cannot do

What it can doWhat it cannot do

The glow people notice after the haldi ceremony is mostly clean, freshly rinsed skin plus a little yellow tint from the turmeric. It is real, but it lasts a day, not weeks.

A simple ubtan for the face

  1. Mix 2 teaspoons of besan, a small pinch of haldi (no more than a quarter teaspoon) and enough plain curd, raw milk or rose water to make a smooth paste.
  2. Optional: half a teaspoon of chandan powder for a cooler feel, or a few drops of plain aloe vera gel for dry skin.
  3. Patch test on the inner arm or jawline 24 hours before, especially if you have never used haldi on your face.
  4. Apply a thin layer on a clean face, away from the eyes and lips.
  5. Leave it for 10 minutes, or until it is just starting to dry. Do not let it crack.
  6. Wet your hands and rinse with plain water, using soft circles with your fingertips. Do not scrub hard.
  7. Pat dry with an old or dark towel, because haldi stains.

The right order: ubtan first, cream after

  1. Wash your face with a gentle face wash such as Gluta Boost Face Wash.
  2. Apply and rinse the ubtan as above.
  3. Wait about 10 minutes for the face to feel fully dry.
  4. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream for the whole face and neck, then a plain moisturiser if your skin still feels tight.
  5. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or higher. Wedding and festival days mean hours in the sun.

Once or twice a week is plenty for ubtan. For the full cream method, see how to use glutathione cream.

Haldi ceremony day: a practical plan

  • The night before: no new products, no scrub, no waxing or threading on the face. Use your usual moisturiser.
  • During the ceremony: relatives often rub haldi paste firmly. Ask for a gentle touch on the face and keep it away from the eyes.
  • Afterwards: rinse with lukewarm water and a gentle face wash. A little yellow tint may stay for a day; do not scrub it off.
  • That night: plain moisturiser only, no retinol, acids or strong actives.
  • Waxing and threading: keep them a few days away from the ceremony; see glutathione cream after waxing and threading.

Who should skip ubtan

  • Sensitive or easily red skin: see glutathione cream for sensitive skin.
  • Active, painful pimples: rubbing a paste over them spreads irritation and can leave marks.
  • Freshly waxed, threaded, shaved or sunburnt skin, or skin peeling from retinol.
  • Anyone allergic to gram flour, turmeric or milk.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if an ubtan or cream leaves your skin itchy, swollen, red or bumpy for more than a day, if dark patches on the cheeks keep spreading, or if acne is painful. Before a wedding, book this a few weeks ahead rather than in the last few days.
