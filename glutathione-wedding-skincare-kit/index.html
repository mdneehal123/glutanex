Wedding skincare kit: an 8 to 12 week glutathione routine for brides and grooms

What a simple wedding skincare kit needs (cleanser, night cream, body wash, sunscreen), a week-by-week plan from 12 weeks out, and what to avoid in the last 2 weeks.

What should a wedding skincare kit have?

Four things cover most needs: a gentle face wash, a night treatment, a body wash and a daily sunscreen. The GlutaNex Glow Trio has the first three in one order; add a sunscreen you already like.

When should a bride or groom start a skincare routine?

8 to 12 weeks before the wedding is a good start. Skin needs a few weeks to settle into a new product, and visible change in tan or marks takes 8 weeks or more with daily sunscreen.

Can I start a new cream one week before the wedding?

It is better not to. A new product, especially one with retinol or acids, can cause dryness or redness while skin adjusts. In the last 2 weeks, keep to products you already use.

A wedding routine works best when it is simple, started early and kept steady. Most brides and grooms need four steps: cleanse, treat at night, care for the body and protect from the sun every morning. Starting 8 to 12 weeks out gives your skin time to adjust and gives tan and marks time to look lighter.

Week-by-week plan

WhenWhat to do
12 to 9 weeks beforeStart the face wash and sunscreen daily. Start the night cream 2 to 3 nights a week, because it has retinol and salicylic acid.
8 to 5 weeks beforeIf skin is comfortable, use the night cream most nights. Book any facial or peel now, not later.
4 to 3 weeks beforeKeep the routine steady. Do waxing or threading at least 3 to 4 days away from the night cream.
Last 2 weeksNo new products, no peels, no new facials. Keep washing, moisturising and wearing sunscreen.
Last 3 nightsSkip the night cream if your skin is at all dry or sensitive; use a plain moisturiser instead.

For grooms

The same plan works. Shaving and the night cream can both irritate, so shave in the morning and apply the cream at night, not on freshly nicked skin. Helmet and bike tan on the forehead and neck responds best to daily sunscreen. See glutathione cream for men.

What to avoid

  • Starting anything new in the last 2 weeks.
  • Lemon, bleach creams or "instant fair" packs: they irritate and can leave patches.
  • Layering retinol with other retinol, AHA or BHA products on the same night.
  • Skipping sunscreen on haldi, mehendi and outdoor days.

Pregnant or breastfeeding? Do not use the night cream (it contains retinol) unless your doctor approves. See also our 12-week wedding plan.
