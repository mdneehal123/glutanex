Glutathione cream with sandalwood (chandan): how to use both safely

Can you use a chandan face pack with glutathione cream? The right order, why not to mix chandan powder or oil into the cream, who should skip it, and what sandalwood can and cannot do.

Can I use sandalwood with glutathione cream?

Yes, as separate steps. Use a plain chandan powder pack mixed with water or rose water on clean skin, wash it off before it dries hard, pat dry, then apply the glutathione cream. Do not mix chandan powder or sandalwood oil into the jar, and patch test first.

Should I apply chandan before or after glutathione cream?

Before, and wash it off. Chandan is used as a wash-off pack. A dry powder layer on top of the cream absorbs it, cracks and flakes onto the pillow.

Does chandan with glutathione make skin fair?

No. Chandan packs can make skin feel cool and fresh and help it look less oily for a while. A glutathione cream with daily sunscreen may help tan and dark marks look lighter slowly over 8 to 12 weeks. Neither changes your natural skin colour.

You can use sandalwood with glutathione cream, but as two separate steps: a plain chandan pack on clean skin first, washed off before it dries hard, and the cream after that. Chandan works as a cooling wash-off pack, while the cream is meant to stay on, so mixing chandan powder or sandalwood oil into the jar is not a good idea.

Chandan has been part of Indian skin care for generations, from haldi-chandan ubtan before weddings to simple packs with gulab jal in summer. Many "chandan" products are also sold with fairness claims. This guide explains the right order, what to mix it with, what to avoid, and what sandalwood can honestly do.

The right order on a chandan night

  1. Cleanse with a gentle face wash such as Gluta Boost Face Wash and pat dry.
  2. Mix the pack: about one teaspoon of pure chandan powder with enough plain water or rose water to make a smooth paste. Avoid lemon.
  3. Apply a thin layer with clean fingers, away from the eyes and lips.
  4. Wash it off after about 10 minutes, while it is still slightly damp. Letting it crack pulls moisture out of the skin.
  5. Rinse with lukewarm water using soft circles. Do not rub the drying paste like a scrub.
  6. Pat dry, wait a few minutes, then apply the glutathione cream as a thin, pea-sized layer.
  7. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or higher.

Once a week is enough for most people, twice in hot, sticky months. For the general layering rules, see how to use glutathione cream. If you like gulab jal in your pack, read rose water with glutathione cream too.

Who should skip chandan or be careful

  • Very dry skin in winter: clay-like packs can make it tighter. Keep it short or skip it until the weather warms.
  • Sensitive skin or fragrance allergy: patch test on the jawline first; sandalwood's natural scent can irritate some people. See glutathione cream for sensitive skin.
  • Oily, acne-prone skin: a plain chandan pack is usually fine occasionally; rinse well and stop if you get new bumps.
  • Broken, sunburnt or freshly waxed skin: skip both the pack and the Night Cream until the skin settles.

What to expect, honestly

A chandan pack can leave skin feeling cool and fresh and looking less shiny for a few hours, which is why it is popular in summer and before functions. It does not fade dark spots or tan on its own and does not change skin colour, whatever a pack's label says. Any gradual change in tan or marks comes from steady use of the cream plus daily sunscreen over 8 to 12 weeks; read glutathione cream results for realistic expectations.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if a pack leaves you with an itchy rash, swelling or red patches that do not settle within a day, or if you are trying to deal with melasma, which needs a proper diagnosis and plan.
