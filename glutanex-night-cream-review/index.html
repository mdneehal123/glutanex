How long does GlutaNex Night Cream take to work?

Skin often feels smoother within a few weeks, but a more even-looking tone usually needs 8 to 12 weeks of regular night use with sunscreen every morning. Results vary.

Who should not use GlutaNex Night Cream?

Who should skip it or ask a doctor first

  • Pregnant or breastfeeding women.
  • Very sensitive, broken or sunburnt skin.
  • Anyone who reacts to retinol or salicylic acid.
  • Anyone with eczema, rosacea or a skin condition.

Teenagers should read glutathione cream for teenagers first. For sensitive skin, see glutathione cream for sensitive skin.

How to use it

  1. Patch test on the jaw or inner arm and wait 24 to 48 hours.
  2. At night, wash your face and pat it dry.
  3. Apply a pea-sized amount as the last step.
  4. Start 2 to 3 nights a week, then increase if your skin is calm.
  5. Wear sunscreen of SPF 30 or higher every morning.

What results to expect

A glutathione cream used with daily sunscreen may help tan and dark marks look lighter slowly over 8 to 12 weeks. It does not change your natural skin colour, and it does not give fairness in days. See glutathione cream results.
