Kya glutathione se gora hota hai?

Nahi, glutathione aapka natural skin colour nahi badalta. Yeh dhoop ke tan, dark patches aur uneven tone ko dheere dheere halka aur ek jaisa dikhane mein madad kar sakta hai, woh bhi daily sunscreen ke saath 8 se 12 hafte mein.

Kya glutathione injection se permanent gora ho sakte hain?

Nahi. Permanent gora karne wala koi bhi injection, tablet ya cream proven nahi hai. Glutathione injections ka skin lightening ke liye koi proper trial nahi hai aur regulators ne inke baare mein warning di hai.

Phir tan halka kaise hota hai?

Tan dhoop se bana extra melanin hai. Glutathione naye melanin ko dheema kar sakta hai, aur sunscreen naya tan banne se rokti hai. Dono saath mein tan ko dheere dheere halka dikhate hain.

In claims se savdhaan

  • "7 din mein gora": aisi creams mein steroid ya mercury ho sakta hai.
  • "Permanent whitening": sunscreen chhodte hi tan wapas aata hai.
  • Bina label ki "fairness" cream: skin ko lambe samay tak nuksan ho sakta hai.

Sahi umeed kya rakhein?

Fresh, even aur healthy dikhne wali skin. Har skin tone sundar hai; glutathione ka kaam rang badalna nahi, skin ko behtar dikhana hai.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawal

Gora hone ke liye sabse safe tarika kya hai?

Natural rang badalna safe ya zaroori nahi hai. Tan aur dark spots ke liye daily sunscreen, halki glutathione ya niacinamide cream aur dheeraj sabse safe tarika hai.

Kya glutathione cream black skin ko white kar sakti hai?

Nahi. Yeh natural rang nahi badalti; sirf tan aur uneven tone ko halka dikhane mein madad kar sakti hai.

Sources
  1. Watanabe F, et al. Skin-whitening and skin-condition-improving effects of topical oxidized glutathione: a double-blind and placebo-controlled clinical trial in healthy women. Clin Cosmet Investig Dermatol. 2014.
  2. US Food and Drug Administration warning on injectable skin-lightening products, 2015 (as reported by MedEsthetics).
