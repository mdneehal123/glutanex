Glutathione injection safe hai kya?

Skin gori karne ke liye glutathione injections ko safe ya asardaar saabit karne wala koi proper clinical trial nahi hai, aur health regulators ne inke baare mein warning di hai. Bina license wali jagah drip se infection ka risk bhi hai. Inse bachna behtar hai.

Glutathione cream, tablet aur injection mein kya behtar hai?

Zyada tar logon ke liye raat ko glutathione cream aur subah sunscreen sabse kam risk wala option hai. Tablets doctor ki salah se hi lein. Skin lightening ke liye injections ki salah nahi di jaati.

Injections ke risk

  • Skin lightening ke liye koi proper trial nahi
  • Allergic reaction aur serious side effects ki reports
  • Ganda needle ya bina license wala clinic: infection ka risk
  • Bottle par kya likha hai aur andar kya hai, pata nahi hota

Warning padhein: US FDA warning, 2015.

Shaadi se pehle injection?

Shaadi se pehle "glow drip" packages bahut bechte hain. Behtar hai 10 se 12 hafte pehle cream aur sunscreen se shuru karein.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawal

Glutathione injection ka asar kitne din rehta hai?

Koi proven asar nahi hai, aur jo bhi farak dikhe woh dhoop mein wapas aa jaata hai. Isliye injections ki salah nahi di jaati.

Kya doctor glutathione injection dete hain?

Kuch clinics dete hain, lekin skin lightening ke liye iska koi approved use nahi hai. Kisi bhi injection se pehle dermatologist se risk samjhein.

Sources
