Glutathione ke kya nuksan hain?

Glutathione cream aam taur par safe hai; shuru mein halki chubhan, redness ya dryness ho sakti hai. Tablets se kabhi kabhi pet mein gas ya ulti jaisa lag sakta hai. Sabse zyada risk injections mein hai, jinke liye skin lightening mein koi proper trial nahi hai.

Kya glutathione cream safe hai?

Zyada tar adults ke liye haan. Pehle 24 ghante kaan ke peeche ya andar ki baanh par patch test karein. Retinol wali cream pregnancy aur breastfeeding mein na lagayein, aur jalan ya sujan ho to use band karein.

Cream ke side effects

  • Shuru mein halki chubhan ya garmi lagna
  • Thodi redness ya dryness, khaaskar retinol wali cream se
  • Bahut kam case mein allergy: khujli, rash

Halki problem ho to cream ek din chhod kar lagayein aur moisturiser use karein.

Tablets ke side effects

Short studies mein tablets zyada tar theek rahe, kuch logon ko pet mein gas ya bloating hui. Lambe time ka use achhe se study nahi hua, isliye doctor se poochkar hi lein.

Injections: sabse zyada risk

Skin gori karne ke liye glutathione injections ka koi proper clinical trial nahi hai, aur regulators ne warning di hai (US FDA warning, 2015). Bina license wali jagah drip se infection ka bhi risk hai. Inse bachein.

Kab turant doctor ke paas jaayein?

Saans lene mein dikkat, chehre ya hothon par sujan, ya tezi se failta rash ho to turant medical madad lein.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawal

Kya glutathione se kidney kharab hoti hai?

Cream se aisa koi saboot nahi hai. Injections ke saath kuch serious reports aaye hain, isliye injections avoid karein aur tablets doctor ki salah se hi lein.

Glutathione cream se pimples ho sakte hain?

Bhaari formula se kuch logon ko ho sakte hain. Halki, non-greasy cream patli layer mein lagayein; salicylic acid pores saaf rakhne mein madad karta hai.

Sources
  1. Watanabe F, et al. Skin-whitening and skin-condition-improving effects of topical oxidized glutathione: a double-blind and placebo-controlled clinical trial in healthy women. Clin Cosmet Investig Dermatol. 2014.
  2. US Food and Drug Administration warning on injectable skin-lightening products, 2015 (as reported by MedEsthetics).

Yeh general cosmetic skincare jaankari hai, medical salah nahi. Koi skin problem ho to dermatologist se milein.
