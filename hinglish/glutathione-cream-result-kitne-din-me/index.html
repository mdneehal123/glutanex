Glutathione cream result kitne din me dikhta hai?

Zyada tar logon ko 1 se 2 hafte mein skin soft aur moist lagti hai, 4 se 8 hafte mein thodi fresh aur bright dikhti hai, aur tan ya uneven tone 8 se 12 hafte mein halka dikhta hai. Iske liye roz raat use aur subah sunscreen zaroori hai. Results har insaan mein alag hote hain.

Glutathione cream se 7 din mein result milta hai?

7 din mein skin thodi soft lag sakti hai, lekin tan ya dark spots mein asli farak nahi aata. Jo cream 7 din mein rang badalne ka daava kare, usmein steroid ya mercury ho sakta hai. Aisi creams se bachein.

Glutathione cream ka asar kyun nahi dikh raha?

Sabse aam wajah: sunscreen na lagana, beech mein cream band karna, ya 8 hafte se pehle hi umeed chhod dena. Melasma jaise patches ya purane gehre daag cream se kam hi badalte hain. 3 mahine baad bhi koi farak na ho to dermatologist ko dikhayein.

Hafte-dar-hafte kya umeed karein

Studies kya kehti hain?

Ek chhoti, placebo-controlled study (Watanabe et al., 2014) mein 30 mahilaon ne 10 hafte tak 2% glutathione lotion lagaya. Glutathione wali side par skin melanin thoda kam hua aur skin zyada moist aur smooth rahi. Matlab asar hota hai, lekin modest aur hafton mein, dinon mein nahi. Badi studies abhi bhi kam hain.

Result kis cheez par depend karta hai?

  • Sunscreen: sabse bada factor. Bina sunscreen naya tan banta rehta hai aur cream ka fayda dikhta nahi.
  • Regular use: hafte mein kabhi kabhi lagane se kam farak padta hai.
  • Daag ka type: tan sabse jaldi halka hota hai; pimple ke daag usse dheere; melasma ko doctor ki zaroorat hoti hai (melasma guide).
  • Dhoop kitni lagti hai: roz bike ya dhoop mein kaam karne walon ko 2 se 3 ghante mein sunscreen dobara lagani chahiye.
  • Pimples: naye pimples aate rahenge to naye daag bhi bante rahenge (acne marks guide).

Progress kaise check karein

  1. Shuru karne se pehle ek photo lein, khidki ki roshni mein, bina makeup aur bina filter.
  2. Har 2 hafte mein usi jagah, usi roshni mein dobara photo lein.
  3. Roz aaine mein na dekhein; dheere hone wala farak roz nahi dikhta.
  4. 8 hafte baad photos ko saath rakh kar dekhein.

Result jaldi lane ke liye kya NA karein

  • Zyada cream lagana ya din mein do baar retinol cream lagana.
  • Ek saath kai strong actives ya scrub.
  • Nimbu, baking soda ya bleach.
  • Glutathione injections; inke skin lightening ke liye proper trial nahi hain aur regulators ne warning di hai (US FDA warning, 2015).

Glutathione cream kitne mahine lagani chahiye?

Kam se kam 2 se 3 mahine regular use karke dekhein. Uske baad bhi routine aur sunscreen chalu rakhne se result bana rehta hai.

Cream band karne ke baad kya tan wapas aa jaata hai?

Dhoop mein bina sunscreen rehne par naya tan ban sakta hai. Isliye sunscreen ki aadat cream se bhi zyada zaroori hai.

Kya face wash se bhi result milta hai?

Face wash skin ko saaf rakhta hai aur routine ka achha hissa hai, lekin chehre par kam der rehta hai. Asli kaam raat ki cream aur subah ki sunscreen karti hai.
