Glutathione cream ke kya fayde hain?

Glutathione cream skin ko antioxidant protection deti hai aur regular use se tan, dull skin aur uneven tone ko halka aur even dikhane mein madad karti hai. Best result daily sunscreen ke saath 8 se 12 hafte mein dikhta hai. Yeh natural rang nahi badalti.

Glutathione cream kitne din mein asar karti hai?

Zyada tar logon ko 4 se 8 hafte mein skin thodi fresh aur bright dikhti hai, aur tan 8 se 12 hafte mein halka hota hai. Iske liye roz raat ko use aur subah sunscreen zaroori hai. Results har insaan mein alag hote hain.

Kin problems mein madad milti hai?

  • Sun tan: dhoop se hua tan dheere dheere halka dikh sakta hai.
  • Dull skin: skin zyada fresh aur glowing dikhti hai.
  • Uneven tone aur dark patches: rang zyada ek jaisa dikh sakta hai.
  • Antioxidant protection: dhoop aur pollution se hone wale damage se bachav.

Studies kya kehti hain?

Chhoti studies mein 4 se 12 hafte mein skin melanin mein thodi, dheere dheere kami dekhi gayi. Matlab asar hota hai, lekin modest aur gradual.

Saath mein kaunse ingredients achhe hain?

Niacinamide, vitamin C aur hyaluronic acid glutathione ke saath achha kaam karte hain. GlutaNex Glutathione Night Cream mein yeh sab hain, saath mein retinol, ceramides aur peptides bhi.

Kya nahi hota?

Glutathione cream aapka natural skin colour nahi badalti, aur sunscreen ke bina result tik nahi paata. 7 din mein gora karne wale claims se bachein.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawal

Kya glutathione cream roz laga sakte hain?

Haan, zyada tar log roz raat ko laga sakte hain. Retinol wali cream ho to pehle 2 hafte, hafte mein 2 se 3 raat se shuru karein.

Kya glutathione cream oily skin par chalegi?

Haan, halki aur non-greasy cream patli layer mein lagayein. Niacinamide ya salicylic acid wali cream oily skin ke liye achhi rehti hai.
