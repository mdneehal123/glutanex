Body tan removal routine: arms, neck, back and feet

Why body tan lasts longer than face tan, a daily routine for arms, neck, back and feet, what a body wash can and cannot do, and how long it takes.

How can I remove tan from my arms and neck?

Protect the skin first: sunscreen or clothing on exposed areas every day. Then cleanse gently, moisturise, and exfoliate gently once or twice a week. Tan usually fades over 4 to 8 weeks once new sun exposure stops.

Does a glutathione body wash remove tan?

Not on its own. A body wash is rinsed off within a minute, so its job is cleansing and a soft, fresh feel. Fading tan depends mostly on stopping new sun exposure.

Why does body tan last longer than face tan?

Body skin is thicker and renews more slowly, and arms, neck and feet often get sun without sunscreen.

Body tan fades when new sun stops reaching the skin; everything else only helps along the way. Arms, neck, the back of the hands and feet tan most because they are exposed daily and are rarely covered by sunscreen.

How long it takes

Light tan often looks better in 3 to 4 weeks, deeper tan in 6 to 12 weeks, as long as you protect the skin daily. Tan lines from straps or watches fade the same way.

What to avoid

  • Lemon, baking soda or bleach on the skin: they irritate and can leave darker patches.
  • Hard scrubbing with stones or harsh scrubs.
  • "Instant whitening" body lotions with unclear ingredients.

When to see a dermatologist

See one for velvety dark patches on the neck or underarms (these can be linked to blood sugar), itchy or spreading patches, or darkening that does not fade after 3 months of sun protection. For underarms and folds, read glutathione for underarms and body.
