The ingredient

What is a glutathione cream?

Glutathione is an antioxidant made of three amino acids that the body produces naturally. In skincare, it is added to creams and face washes made for brighter-looking, more even skin, often alongside ingredients like niacinamide. A glutathione face cream is a leave-on cream, the format used in research, which is why it does more than a wash-off product.

Research on glutathione applied to the skin is still limited but encouraging: a small 2014 placebo-controlled study of a 2% glutathione lotion found a modest, gradual improvement in skin tone over 10 weeks. Consistent use and daily sunscreen matter more than any single product.

Before you order

What is glutathione?

Glutathione is an antioxidant made of three amino acids, found naturally in the body. In skincare it is used in creams and face washes made for brighter-looking, more even skin.

How do I use the GlutaNex Night Cream?

At night, wash your face with a gentle cleanser (the Gluta Boost Face Wash is launching soon), pat dry and apply a small amount of the cream to the face and neck. Use sunscreen every morning.

When will I see a difference?

Skin changes slowly. Use your routine every night for at least 6 to 8 weeks, with daily sunscreen, before judging. Results vary from person to person.
