Glutathione cream for summer tan: a simple routine

How to protect your skin in Indian summers and help tan fade faster.

By the GlutaNex team · Updated 11 October 2026 · 1 sources cited

Key takeaways
  • Daily SPF 30+ sunscreen, reapplied outdoors, does most of the work against tan.
  • A glutathione night cream can help tan look lighter within a few weeks.
  • Avoid lemon, baking soda and bleach creams on tanned skin.
On this page
  1. Daytime: protect
  2. Night: care
  3. How long tan takes to fade
  4. Avoid

Summer tan fades on its own once you protect your skin; a glutathione night cream can help it look lighter sooner. Sun protection does most of the work.

Daytime: protect

  • Broad-spectrum sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher with a high PA rating (PA+++ or more).
  • Use enough: about two finger-lengths for face and neck.
  • Reapply every 2 to 3 hours outdoors, and after sweating.
  • Cap, sunglasses and shade at midday.

Night: care

Cleanse with Gluta Boost Face Wash, then apply GlutaNex Glutathione Night Cream. On sunburnt or peeling skin, pause actives and use a plain moisturiser until it calms.

How long tan takes to fade

Usually a few weeks once you protect your skin consistently; deep tan from months outdoors can take longer.

Avoid

Lemon juice, baking soda, harsh scrubs and bleach creams: they irritate skin and can leave it patchy.

Frequently asked questions

How can I remove summer tan fast?

Protect your skin daily with SPF 30+ sunscreen and reapply outdoors; a glutathione night cream can help tan look lighter within a few weeks.

Can I use glutathione cream on sunburnt skin?

Wait until sunburn has healed; use a plain, soothing moisturiser meanwhile.
