Can I use castor oil with glutathione cream?

Yes, on different areas. Castor oil is a very thick oil that many people use on eyebrows, cracked heels, cuticles and dry elbows. A glutathione face cream goes on the clean face at night. Avoid layering castor oil over an active face cream; it is heavy, can clog pores and may make stinging worse.

Should I apply castor oil before or after glutathione cream?

On the face, neither is ideal. Oil underneath stops the cream spreading evenly, and thick oil on top seals in an active cream. If you use castor oil on your brows at night, apply the face cream first, let it settle, then dab the oil only on the brow hairs.

Does castor oil with glutathione make skin fair?

No. Castor oil can help very dry patches feel softer. A glutathione cream with daily sunscreen may help tan and dark marks look lighter slowly over 8 to 12 weeks. Neither changes your natural skin colour.

You can use castor oil and glutathione cream in the same routine, but keep them on different areas: castor oil for eyebrows, cracked heels, cuticles and dry elbows, and the glutathione cream as a thin layer on your clean face at night. Do not mix castor oil into the cream, and do not layer it over an active face cream; it is one of the thickest oils there is and can feel heavy and clog pores.

Arandi ka tel is kept in many Indian homes for hair, brows, lashes and heels, and some people now rub it on the face for dark circles or dry skin. This guide explains where it fits next to a glutathione cream, who should skip it on the face, and what to expect honestly.

A simple night routine using both

  1. Wash your face with a gentle face wash such as Gluta Boost Face Wash and pat it dry.
  2. Apply the glutathione cream as a thin, pea-sized layer on the face and neck, away from the eyes and lips.
  3. Wait 10 to 15 minutes so the cream settles.
  4. Castor oil only where it belongs: a tiny drop on a clean spoolie or cotton bud for the brow hairs, a few drops on cracked heels (then cotton socks), or a little on cuticles and elbows.
  5. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or higher.

Why castor oil is not a great face oil

  • It is very heavy. On oily, combination or acne-prone skin, castor oil left overnight can lead to bumps and clogged-feeling pores, especially in humid weather.
  • It traps what is underneath. Over a cream with retinol and salicylic acid, a thick seal of oil can make tingling feel stronger.
  • It is sticky. It picks up dust and hair and leaves marks on the pillow.
  • It can irritate some people. A small number react to castor oil itself. Patch test on the inner arm for two days first.

If your face feels tight in winter, a richer moisturiser is usually kinder. See glutathione cream for dry skin, and compare lighter oils in glutathione cream with almond oil and glutathione cream with coconut oil.

Castor oil under the eyes: an honest view

Castor oil is often shared as a dark circle remedy. It can make the thin skin under the eyes feel less dry, but it does not fade dark circles, which usually come from genes, thin skin, shadows, allergies or tiredness. Oil near the eyes can also run into them and cause blurring or irritation. Do not put a glutathione cream with retinol close to the eyes either. How to pick and store castor oil

  • Choose plain castor oil in a sealed bottle, without added perfume or colour. Cold-pressed oil is a common choice for skin and hair use.
  • Check the smell and look. It should smell mild and look clear to pale yellow. If it smells sharp or rancid, throw it away.
  • Keep the cap closed and the bottle out of direct sun, away from the stove.
  • Use a clean dropper or cotton bud instead of dipping fingers into the bottle.
  • Keep it away from children; castor oil is not something to swallow without a doctor's advice.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if your skin burns, swells or breaks into a rash after castor oil, if pimples on the face get worse, if cracked heels bleed or do not get better, or if dark patches keep spreading. Those need a proper diagnosis, not more oil.
