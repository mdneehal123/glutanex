Glutathione cream with beetroot (chukandar): how to use them safely

Can you use beetroot with glutathione cream? Yes, as a separate rinse-off pack or lip tint, never mixed into the jar. The right order, what beetroot can and cannot do, mixes to skip, staining tips and the night-only retinol rule.

Can I mix beetroot juice with glutathione cream?

It is better not to. Mixing fresh juice into a cream dilutes it, can spoil the jar and adds bacteria. Use beetroot as a separate rinse-off pack, wash it off, pat dry, then apply a thin layer of cream on its own.

Does beetroot make skin glow?

A beetroot pack can leave skin looking fresh and slightly pink for a short time, mostly because of its natural red colour. There is no reliable evidence that it fades dark spots or changes skin tone. Daily sunscreen and a steady routine matter far more.

Can I apply beetroot on lips with glutathione cream?

Yes, a little beetroot juice works as a temporary natural lip tint. Keep any retinol night cream, including GlutaNex Night Cream, off the lips, and use a plain lip balm at night instead.

You can use beetroot and glutathione cream in the same routine, but not mixed together. Use beetroot as a short rinse-off pack or a lip tint, wash it off, let your face dry, and then apply a thin layer of glutathione cream on its own.

Chukandar juice and beetroot packs are popular home tips in India for a "pink glow", especially in winter and wedding season. Beetroot is a food, not a skin treatment, so it helps to know what it can do, what it cannot, and how to keep it from irritating skin or clashing with a night cream.

Drinking beetroot juice is a food choice. It is fine as part of a balanced diet, but it will not replace sunscreen or skincare; read glutathione rich foods for an honest view on diet.

The right order: beetroot pack first, cream after

  1. Wash your face with a gentle face wash such as Gluta Boost Face Wash.
  2. Make a simple pack: 1 to 2 teaspoons of fresh beetroot juice with a spoon of plain curd or aloe vera gel, or a little besan for a thicker paste.
  3. Apply a thin layer, away from the eyes. Leave it for 5 to 10 minutes. Do not let it dry hard and crack.
  4. Rinse with plain water and pat dry with a dark or old towel, because beetroot stains.
  5. Wait about 10 minutes for the face to feel fully dry.
  6. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream for the whole face, then a plain moisturiser if your skin feels tight.
  7. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or higher. This step matters more than any pack.

Beetroot as a lip and cheek tint

A drop of beetroot juice dabbed on lips or cheeks gives a soft, natural tint for a few hours. It is a cosmetic colour, not a fix for dark lips.

  • Patch test on the inner arm first; some people react to beetroot.
  • For lips, use a plain lip balm over it. Do not apply any retinol night cream on the lips.
  • For dark or pigmented lips, read glutathione for dark lips; lip sunscreen and stopping lip-licking help more than any tint.
  • Make fresh juice each time. Do not store it for days.

Who should skip beetroot packs

  • Sensitive or easily red skin: the pink tint can hide redness from a reaction; see glutathione cream for sensitive skin.
  • Freshly waxed, threaded or shaved skin, or skin that is peeling from retinol.
  • Anyone with a food allergy to beetroot.
  • Active, painful acne: ask a dermatologist rather than adding packs.

Glow and tan: an honest view

A beetroot pack gives a short-lived fresh look and a pink tint. It does not remove tan, fade dark spots or change your natural skin colour, and no home pack or cream can promise that. Tan and marks look lighter slowly, over weeks, with daily sunscreen and a steady routine. For dull winter skin, read glutathione for dull skin; for other kitchen tips, see glutathione cream with tomato.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist if a pack leaves your skin itchy, swollen or red for more than a day, if dark patches on the cheeks keep spreading, or if acne is painful or leaves deep marks.
