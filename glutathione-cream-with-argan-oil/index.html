Glutathione cream with argan oil: the right order, how many drops, and what argan oil can and cannot do

Can I use argan oil with glutathione cream?

Yes, if your skin is dry, normal or dry on the cheeks. Apply the glutathione cream first on clean, dry skin, wait a few minutes, then press one or two drops of pure argan oil over dry areas. Use it at night, and do not mix the oil into the cream jar.

Should argan oil go before or after cream?

After. An oil forms a thin layer that slows water loss, so it works best as the last step. Applying argan oil first makes the cream slide around and can leave the face greasy without helping either product.

Does argan oil remove dark spots or tan?

No. Argan oil helps dry skin feel softer and look less dull, but it does not remove dark spots, tan or acne marks, and it does not change skin colour. Marks fade slowly over weeks, mainly with daily sunscreen and a steady routine.

You can use argan oil with glutathione cream, as long as you layer them instead of mixing them. Apply the cream first on clean, dry skin, wait a few minutes, then press one or two drops of pure argan oil over the dry parts of your face as the last step of your night routine.

Argan oil is pressed from the kernels of the argan tree, which grows in Morocco. It feels medium-light, absorbs fairly well and is more stable in heat than many delicate oils. In India it is sold mostly for hair, and often as "liquid gold" for the face. It can make dry skin feel softer and look less dull, but it is not a brightening treatment.

The right order: cream first, argan oil last

  1. Wash your face with a gentle face wash such as Gluta Boost Face Wash and pat dry.
  2. Wait until skin is fully dry, especially on nights you use a retinol cream. Damp skin can make retinol sting more.
  3. Apply a pea-sized amount of glutathione cream over the face and neck.
  4. Wait 5 to 10 minutes so the cream settles.
  5. Warm one or two drops of argan oil between your fingertips and press them over dry areas: cheeks, sides of the nose, jawline. Skip an oily forehead and nose.
  6. Next morning: face wash, a light moisturiser if needed, and a broad-spectrum sunscreen of SPF 30 or more.

Two drops is enough for most faces; more only makes the pillow oily. For the full cream method, read how to use glutathione cream.

Is your argan oil pure? A quick label check

Many bottles sold as argan oil in India are hair serums or blends with a little argan in them, plus silicones, mineral oil or fragrance. These are fine for hair but not what you want on your face at night.

  • The ingredient list should say only "Argania spinosa kernel oil".
  • No added fragrance: pure cosmetic argan oil has a mild, slightly nutty smell or almost none.
  • Dark glass or opaque bottle with a dropper, a batch number and a use-by date.
  • A very low price for a large bottle is a warning sign that it is diluted.
  • Hair serum is not face oil. If the label says "hair", leave it for your hair.

Keep the oil and the cream cool and closed, away from a sunny window; see how to store glutathione cream.

Argan vs other face oils

Argan sits in the middle: lighter than coconut or castor oil, a little richer than jojoba, and more stable in heat than rosehip. You only need one oil, not several. Compare jojoba oil, rosehip oil and almond oil with glutathione cream if you are choosing one for winter. Coconut oil is heavy for acne-prone faces; see coconut oil with glutathione cream.

Argan oil on lips, hands and heels

Argan oil is also useful away from the face. Rub a drop into cuticles and dry hands at night, or over a thick foot cream on cracked heels. Keep any glutathione face cream with retinol off your lips; for dry lips, a plain lip balm or a tiny drop of argan oil works better.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist for acne that is painful or cystic, for brown patches on both cheeks that may be melasma, or if any oil causes itching, rash or swelling. Patch test new oils on your jawline first.
