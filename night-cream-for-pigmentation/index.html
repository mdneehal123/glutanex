Night cream for pigmentation: ingredients that help and a routine that works

Which night cream ingredients help pigmentation, dark spots and tan look lighter, a simple routine for Indian skin, realistic timelines, and when pigmentation needs a dermatologist.

Which night cream is good for pigmentation?

Look for a night cream with niacinamide, plus one or more of glutathione, vitamin C, retinol, alpha arbutin or azelaic acid, and hydrators such as ceramides. Use it consistently at night and wear sunscreen every morning, because sun exposure is the main reason pigmentation returns.

How long does a night cream take to reduce pigmentation?

Most people notice dark spots and tan looking lighter after 8 to 12 weeks of nightly use with daily sunscreen. Older spots and melasma take longer, may only lighten partly, and often need a dermatologist.

Can a night cream remove pigmentation completely?

Usually not completely. A good night cream can help pigmentation look lighter and more even, but deep or hormonal pigmentation such as melasma often needs a dermatologist's treatment and strict sun protection.

A night cream can help pigmentation look lighter, but only alongside daily sunscreen. Pigmentation is extra melanin, and sunlight keeps triggering more of it. The best approach is a night cream with proven ingredients, sunscreen every morning, and patience for 8 to 12 weeks.

Night cream ingredients that help pigmentation

  • Niacinamide: helps reduce the transfer of pigment to the skin surface; gentle and well studied. See glutathione vs niacinamide.
  • Glutathione: antioxidant used for brighter, more even-looking tone. See glutathione for pigmentation.
  • Retinol: speeds up skin renewal so pigmented surface cells shed sooner. Start slowly.
  • Vitamin C: antioxidant that helps dullness and dark marks. See glutathione vs vitamin C.
  • Alpha arbutin, kojic acid, azelaic acid, tranexamic acid: other common brightening actives; see alpha arbutin and tranexamic acid.
  • Ceramides and hyaluronic acid: keep the skin barrier healthy, so actives cause less irritation. Irritation itself can leave dark marks on Indian skin.

Mistakes that make pigmentation worse

  • Skipping sunscreen on cloudy days, indoors near windows, or on two-wheeler rides.
  • Lemon, baking soda or bleach on the face. See glutathione cream with lemon.
  • Scrubbing or picking at spots and pimples.
  • Steroid or unlabelled "fairness" creams, which can thin the skin and cause a flare when stopped. See glutathione vs hydroquinone.
  • Using too many actives at once, causing irritation and new dark marks.

Realistic timeline

  • 4 weeks: skin looks less dull; tan starts to lighten if you use sunscreen.
  • 8 to 12 weeks: acne marks and dark spots may look noticeably lighter.
  • 3 to 6 months: older spots may lighten partly. Melasma needs ongoing care.

When to see a dermatologist

See a dermatologist for patchy pigmentation on both cheeks, dark velvety skin on the neck or underarms, spots that change shape, colour or bleed, or no change after 3 months of a good routine. These may need prescription treatment or a check for hormonal causes such as PCOS.
