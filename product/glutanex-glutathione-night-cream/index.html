How to use

Use at night only. Cleanse and pat your face dry, then smooth a pea-sized amount over the face and neck, avoiding the eyes and lips.

Start slowly: this cream contains retinol and salicylic acid, so use it 2 to 3 nights a week for the first two weeks, then every night if your skin is comfortable. Mild tingling or dryness at the start is common; stop if redness or burning continues.

Every morning, use a broad-spectrum sunscreen, because retinol and acids make skin more sensitive to the sun.

Do not use it on the same night as other retinol, AHA/BHA or peel products. Patch test on a small area for 24 hours before first use. Not for use during pregnancy or breastfeeding unless your doctor says it is fine.

When will I see results?

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Patch test first on a small area for 24 hours. If redness, itching or burning continues, stop and speak to a dermatologist.

Can I use it during pregnancy?

The Night Cream contains retinol (vitamin A) and salicylic acid. Do not use it during pregnancy or breastfeeding unless your doctor says it is fine.

Why start 2 to 3 nights a week?

Retinol and salicylic acid can cause mild dryness or tingling while skin gets used to them. Starting slowly and using sunscreen every morning keeps it comfortable.
